# ADR-016 — Synthetic thermal hemisphere policy and signed annual metadata

Date: 2026-11-03. Implementation: implemented for P2-T5/T6/T7 below. Verification: see continuation receipt. Release: blocked for full Phase2/model adoption. This is **not Phase2 completion**.

## Decision and provenance

The existing synthetic 8760-hour thermal screen used the northern day-200 temperature cosine at every latitude. Its solar proxy already used signed latitude, so southern summer irradiation was paired with a northern winter temperature cycle. The annual temperature component is now `sign(latitude) * cos(2*pi*(day-200)/365)`: retain the existing north timing, reverse the annual harmonic in the south, and use zero hemispheric annual temperature harmonic at exactly zero latitude. The equator policy is deliberate, not a claim that equatorial climates have no annual temperature/rainfall cycle. Latitude inputs must be supplied and within ±90°. The inherited ±89.9° solar-proxy clamp remains unchanged for polar numerical behavior.

NASA/JPL explains opposite hemispheric seasonal timing: https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/seasons/en/ (updated 2025-07-29; fetched in this continuation). That source does **not** establish this application's day-200 peak, amplitude, equator rule or cloud/temperature distribution. Those remain qualified local policies. No external annual weather, TMY or model-quality benchmark is claimed.

The integrator retains the existing solar-proxy weights normalized to `PSH * 365` kWh/m² per year, min/max temperature clipping, NOCT equation and mount offsets. Independently vectorized Python/numpy reconstructs the declared profile; installed pvlib0.13.1 executes the DC temperature response. Source: https://pvlib-python.readthedocs.io/en/v0.13.1/reference/generated/pvlib.pvsystem.pvwatts_dc.html . For each hour the reference uses `max(0, Pdc)` and integrates energy, consistent with the existing application's nonnegative DC screen. The independent oracle is an implementation/policy check, not an independently validated site-temperature model.

At zero PSH there is no reference generation and a thermal-yield ratio is not physically evaluable. The result now explicitly labels `zero_reference_irradiation` and uses a neutral zero-loss budget rather than the former spurious100% loss. Energy production remains zero. This is a defined zero-denominator convention, not a measured zero thermal loss.

## Acceptance and execution

- **P2-T5:** 32 independent Python/pvlib integration cases cover latitude−90/−35/0/+35/+90, all three mounting policies, NOCT40/42/45 and coefficients−.3/+.3%/°C; plus zero/low-PSH and absent annual mean. Opposite-sign coefficient cases are algebraic tests, not product claims. Southern witness at−35° changes signed thermal loss from approximately−.3969115373% under the independently reconstructed old northern-only phase to+1.2037600357% under the declared corrected policy. The difference demonstrates the original defect; it is not a forecast improvement percentage.
- **P2-T6:** invalid/missing design extremes, nonfinite inputs, out-of-range latitude, reversed extremes, a mean outside design extremes, unknown mount environment and invalid sensitivity allowance reject. Temperatures must be above absolute zero; this lower physical bound does not establish the model's empirical domain. No invented upper temperature/PSH performance domain is asserted. An absent annual mean still uses the explicitly qualified midpoint of design extremes. A supplied mean is now labeled `supplied_annual_mean`, not falsely certified as provider evidence. Direct exported integrator arguments also reject nonfinite mean/bias.
- **P2-T7:** canonical `thermalDeratePctUsed` now means signed loss, the inverse of the signed stage power change. Cold gain is negative loss. A dedicated inverse-sign component with independent fixtures centralizes this conversion; missing stage metadata defaults to neutral zero, not a fabricated thermal measurement. The existing thermal-stage validation is retained. Twelve mount/override combinations verify named waterfall/canonical annual energy and signed metadata agreement, including JSON readback. JSON's normalization of−0 to0 is explicitly accepted; nonzero signs are preserved.

The independent numerical comparison tolerance is absolute1e−9 percentage points plus relative1e−11, for float64/vectorized-versus-sequential accumulation only. It is **not** model/field accuracy. Five newly registered exact-expression fixtures are executed and mutation-sensitive; numerical detection/gate code is unchanged. The generator imports no TypeScript and reads no production numeric outputs. The scalar sign convention fixtures verify definition only, not weather applicability.

## Compatibility, guide and rollback

Southern/equatorial synthetic annual estimates change and must be recomputed from project inputs. New results identify `hemisphere_cosine_equator_flat_v1`. Existing snapshots without the policy label are historical/unspecified; they must not be promoted to current-model outputs. The supplied-mean source string changes from `provider_annual_mean` to `supplied_annual_mean`; report strings/fixtures/core characterization updated, and consumers are checked. Report snapshot remains supplemental, not a new restorable project-input schema or a complete global model-policy migration.

Rollback for annual adoption is to disable the synthetic override at the consuming boundary and retain a clearly labeled scalar/hot-design screening fallback or mark annual assessment unavailable. Do not reinstate northern-only southern seasonality, label an unknown mean as verified provider evidence, clamp away cold gains or restore the false zero-PSH100% budget. Preserve historical snapshots and recompute dependent energy/finance reports after changing the selected policy. No repository reset or unrelated inverse patch is appropriate.
