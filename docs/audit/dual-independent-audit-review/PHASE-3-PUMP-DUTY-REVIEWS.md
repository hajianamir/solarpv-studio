## Review 2 — physical identity, numerical failure and evidence coverage

Checked SI dimensions: rho*g*V*H is joules/day; dividing by3.6e6gives kWh/day, dividing by hours gives mean kW, and each efficiency division transfers to the upstream power boundary. Halving declared runtime doubles mean power without changing work/energy for constant head and efficiencies; changing runtime cannot invent a daily solar resource. Available DC energy is already at the drive input and must not be confused with PV STC kWp or energy before unmodeled losses.

Follow-up independent calculation: standard-library60-digit Decimal computes864Cartesian valid cases from base-unit work and explicit efficiency products. Five additional IEEE754domain witnesses independently require unavailable rather than zero: overflow, erased positive flow, erased positive work/energy, and efficiency-induced overflow. Strict tests reject missing/unknown/type/range/nonfinite values, while zero volume/head/DC availability remain valid. All results refuse guarantee/adoption; available ones retain unassessed equipment/chronological delivery. No energy budget is promoted into a water-feasibility or PV-sizing result.

Initial expression registration failed for the second positive-volume underflow guard: the first tiny-volume/tiny-head vector already failed earlier at erased flow and did not exercise the intended path sensitively. Added a distinct positive-flow/tiny-head work-underflow witness. All18current expressions then executed against independent targets and changed output under mutation. No expression was excluded, guard removed, tolerance widened or expected output generated by production. Focused tests independently inventory all expressions even while new source is untracked.
