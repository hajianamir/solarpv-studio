## Governing identities, domain and evidence

Hydraulic daily energy is rho*g*V*H /3,600,000 kWh. Average hydraulic kW is that energy divided by declared pumping hours. Divide successively by pump hydraulic, motor and drive efficiencies to obtain shaft, motor electrical and DC-input power, then daily energy. No idle/standby/cable/other losses, variable head/flow, transients, dynamics, thermal/current/voltage limits or equipment curves are modeled. These are idealized steady-duty physical identities with user-declared constants, not empirical pump/PV delivery validation. Full precision means IEEE754, not exact arbitrary-precision accounting.
